This module allows generating purchase contracts from purchase orders
through a wizard.

Users can customize contract lines and generate multiple contracts from
the same purchase order, respecting the remaining available quantities.

Generated contracts are linked to their purchase orders and accessible
through a smart button.

Usage
=====

Usage
-----

1. Go to **Purchases > Orders > Purchase Orders** and open a purchase
   order.
2. Click **Generate Agreement** to open the contract generation wizard.
3. Configure the contract name, start date, responsible user, and
   recurrence settings.
4. Review the purchase order lines loaded automatically into the wizard.
5. Modify quantities, descriptions, or unit prices, or remove any lines
   that should not be included.
6. Click **Generate Contract** to create and open the purchase contract.

Multiple Contracts
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Multiple contracts can be generated from the same purchase order.

When reopening the wizard, only the remaining available quantities are
proposed, taking into account quantities already assigned to other
contracts.

Canceled or deleted contract lines release their quantities, while
archived contracts continue to reserve them.

The system prevents contracting quantities greater than those ordered or
reducing purchase order quantities below those already contracted.

Accessing Contracts
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Use the **Contracts** smart button on the purchase order to access its
generated contracts.

Contracts can be further configured using the standard contract form.
